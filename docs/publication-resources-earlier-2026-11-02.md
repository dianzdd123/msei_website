## Attribution and link decisions

- A repository, dataset or movie must be tied to the exact paper by an author project page, the manuscript, the publisher or a matching official README. Broad topic similarity is insufficient.
- The later [Optical-Microrobot-Database](https://lannwei.github.io/Optical-Microrobot-Database/) references several older papers, but that alone does not establish that its data were originally released with those papers. Its resources are left for the separate dataset-paper row.
- The 2026 `Zongcai23/Micro_Object_Sim2Real` project is a different work and is not a resource for the 2022 Communications Physics paper.
- Pretrained models are not automatically labelled datasets. General author homepages are not added as redundant publication buttons.
- An official URL being present is distinct from successful playback. The Bi-Touch demo's association was verified through its embed; automated retrieval was throttled. Wiley and ACS supplementary media are documented from the publisher, with the retrieval limits above.
- Existing bibliographic links were not changed by this research pass. No publication content, figure or renderer file was edited.
