## Image processing and checks

Original panels and in-image labels were kept intact. Processing was limited to decoding/extracting the originals, compositing transparency onto white where necessary, scaling down and encoding WebP. There was no generative editing, relabelling, recolouring, data alteration, panel rearrangement or content-level cropping. All seven final files were visually inspected, and each mapping title was checked against the assigned publication title. The two large square overview figures retain more pixels to keep small text readable; the PixelTac file is approximately 272 KB for that reason.

Source downloads and the extraction manifest are retained in the task workspace under `publication-figure-work/recent-g/`. These are intermediate provenance files, not website assets.
