## Programme-supported corrections

- **UK Manipulation 2025:** the [official programme](https://www.robot-manipulation.uk/_files/ugd/20f657_5540a3e31c5449768caf48cfaad29f3c.pdf), page 7, lists Dandan’s individual title as **Robotic Dexterous Micromanipulation for Biomedical Applications**, at 09:15 on **8 January 2025**, Bush House Auditorium, King’s College London. The old title was the session heading. The workshop ran 7–8 January, rather than May.
- **MedTech Links 2025:** the [Imperial programme](https://www.imperial.ac.uk/media/imperial-college/research-centres-and-groups/medtech-links/Mar25_MedTech-Links-programme_FINAL.pdf) lists Dandan’s AI-based micromanipulation talk on **24 March 2025, 16:45–17:00**, at Royal School of Mines room RSM 2.28. The event title is **MedTech Links: AI, Machine Learning Systems and Devices with Autonomy**. This corrects the old May date and supplies the actual event/venue name.
- **I-X 2024:** the [official Imperial event](https://www.imperial.ac.uk/events/175730/ai-cutting-edge-overviews-and-tutorials-with-dr-dandan-zhang/) confirms **30 April 2024, 15:00–16:00 BST**, the existing individual title, and I-X Conference Room, level 5, I-HUB, White City. This corrects the old December date.
- **UK Manipulation 2026:** the [organiser’s archive](https://www.robot-manipulation.uk/past-workshops) confirms 6–7 January 2026, University of Edinburgh. The retained programme image places Dandan in the Wednesday 7 January dexterity session. The individual title is retained from the owner archive.
- **IROS EAI-EC 2025:** the [official workshop listing](https://2025.ieee-iros.org/WorkshopsTutorials.html) places the workshop on **24 October 2025**, afternoon, room 210D. Its [dedicated workshop page](https://www.csclab.org/eisr) was unavailable during this pass. The existing individual title remains owner-provided.
- **Hamlyn Trustworthy Medical Robotics 2025:** [organiser Mary He’s institutional profile](https://www.salford.ac.uk/our-staff/mary-he) and [institutional recognition record](https://salford-repository.worktribe.com/person/1179439/mary-he/recognition) support **24 June 2025**, Royal Geographical Society, London. The individual title remains from the owner archive.
- **Hamlyn microrobotics 2024:** retain the supplied month and individual title. The official 2024 proceedings cover establishes the event/year context but does not independently verify this individual talk; no exact day is inferred.

## Keep the two iCANX occurrences separate

The [ICEE/Tsinghua report](https://thu-ifee.tsinghua.edu.cn/en/industry/20251103/1775.html) independently confirms an iCANX event at the Hong Kong Convention and Exhibition Centre on **20–21 October 2025**. It does not independently identify Dandan’s individual talk. The owner’s October record is retained, with Hong Kong spelling corrected and a crop of the actual event signage. The source photograph contains another speaker, so the crop deliberately includes signage alone.

The [3 June 2025 ScienceNet announcement](https://news.sciencenet.cn/htmlnews/2025/6/545200.shtm) explicitly identifies Dandan, the multimodal-sensor talk, **iCANX Youth Talks Vol. 100**, and the online programme at **20:00–21:30 Beijing time**. Add this as **`icanx-youth-talks-2025`** with its original poster. A repeated talk title does not establish that the two events are the same occurrence.

The AfricanMRS announcement PDF supplied as a possible October poster source returned a CAPTCHA redirect on direct download. That route was stopped without retries or circumvention; the verified ICEE signage crop was used instead.
