# Publication figure sources and processing

Prepared for the MSEI publication-page update on 1 November 2026.

These 17 thumbnails reproduce figures from the authors’ manuscripts or public project repositories. Each publication card identifies the visual as a manuscript or project figure and links to the source. They are kept distinct from the research-theme illustrations used on other records.

## Processing and attribution

Figures were extracted as intact image objects from the cited manuscript pages or downloaded from author project repositories. PDF soft masks and PNG alpha channels were composited onto white to match the original document presentation. Images were proportionally reduced to at most 1500 × 1300 pixels and encoded as WebP. Scientific content, labels and figure panels were not redrawn, recoloured, rearranged or selectively removed. Figures were visually inspected after conversion.

Copyright and source rights remain with the original authors and publishers. This website adds no blanket licence or relicensing claim to these figures. The associated publication records retain author attribution, and each figure has a source link.

## Source and release details

- Newer article metadata and publication status are maintained independently of the thumbnail source. An author-manuscript figure does not change a journal paper into a preprint.
- MagicGripper uses the hardware diagram from Fig. 5; optical depth estimation uses the focal-depth comparison from Fig. 2; TacVerse uses representative benchmark samples from Fig. 2.
- PixelTac was not downloaded from Imperial’s restricted request-a-copy item. Its figure remains a clearly labelled research-theme illustration unless a separately accessible author or publisher figure is supplied.
- The original downloads and extraction metadata are temporary working files; the website release contains only the optimised figure assets and this provenance note.
